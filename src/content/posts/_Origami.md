title: Does Origami have the power to heal hearts?
pubDate: 2026-07-29
---
I often feel that my life is pleasureful and I am fortunate to have this - to that I have nothing to argue against as I myself feel the same. Born in a well to do family, and been taken care of my basic desires till now is something I should be glad about. This feeling intensifies when I see elsewhere, I see alot of them even struggling for the basic requirements.

Recently I got the chance to experience this to a new extent when my Maternal Grandfather was admitted to the hospital for a brain surgery. Once I got the news about the surgery that he'd get admitted the next day, I got tensed and in these times it's pretty normal as there's only few things in my hand to do, mostly upto the severity of the condition and the effectiveness of the surgery. I also had a past guilt where I was unable to show my emotions and care to my Paternal Grandfather when he was sick during the second wave of CoVID and later ended up not even able to attend his funeral. I knew this time I need to give for the things I have taken and felt as a duty to fulfill. I stayed with him during his surgery days and was a regular visitor during convalescence to cheer him up. Through all these, I got alot of time to let my mind wonders in thoughts. 


Since brain is the most complex part of human body, so does the ways of operating it in surgery, the ward where my grandfather was placed was named neurology department. The first time I went there, I couldn't grasp the fact that what healthy looking people were the patients. After being a regular companion, i also got the chance to interact with them. One of them was a boy with whom I conversed daily, told me that he is losing the sight in his left eye. It wasn't caused by any impact but rather a slit that forms around his optic nerve that is blocking his vision. It was a shocking revelation to me as he seemed the most cheerful among all. My cousin in early days, jokingly passed a comment him being perfectly fine. I thought how unfortunate it'd be to be in the situation and how much courage it's needed to endure it. I asked him politely about how it feels to be in this situation to which he replied energetically. After conversing further, I got to know that my father told him that I am pursuing philosophy and he wanted to talk to me ever since. He liked Stoicism, which of course most males do but that was my first time seeing someone applying the knowledge like this. Diogenes was his favorite and to be honest I expected him to say the name of the founder of Cynicism. I also started my Philosophy reading how Diogenes wipes the floor with Plato and he recalled the same stories to me and for the first time I felt that I am glad that I pursued this course. He showed interest pursuing the same in his masters and asked my experience regarding, to which I told him that academic philosophy is something different than what he'd expect it to be. I told him the gap between my expectations and the reality and urged him towards reading on his own pace and liking rather than forcibly.
