I woke up abruptly early in the morning to a feel uneasy and felt my heart rate was a bit higher than it's used to be as if there's something bothering me unconsciously and the reason for that weird feeling. I tried to sleep back as it was still 2 hrs before than I normal waking up time but no matter how many protocols I tried, I couldn't find myself to rest to sleep and after a while I feel like okay let's gave up and call it a morning. I got a spike urge to going to the park, as to calm me down a bit so I tied up my shoe laces and went for it.

Life has been so uncanny lately, it's like I am in senses to know what's happening around me but still something I couldn't understand what and that must be the reason of abrupt body response as many of times something we miss to register in mind, body tends to remember and gives signal time to time and it fascinates me a whenever I miss the cues as I thought that I could put reasoning to everything happening to me but strangely realising that it's not the case always. 

When I reached the park, I was walking hastily as if I were rushing to be on time which wasn't the case as I took this medium for pleasure and calm my unnerving feeling but by then I realised something was off and then I felt I need to consciously take a pit stop. Here the park where I go regularly, is just so good as if the nature is showing off how charming it could be and it definitely managed to enchant me as If I got attracted to a girl who seemed to possess all the qualities beauty could ask for. And as if the park wanted to keep charming me, it introduced me with cute little ducks and I do not know till when I can keep my hands in pants and not jump to hold them. I sat near the fountain where people are less as I demanded some privacy for my emotions and then I started processing my life through the lens of mine. I usually feel the need of having something in my hands or to touch whenever I feel sort of processing as If it helps to focus a bit. As the process deepens, I felt like I wanted something desperately, but couldn't find a way to tell how to get it as if I were standing in a fog, and found someone out there but unable to tell who and how to reach. 

Through these heaviness of feeling I sat with, I saw a small girl approaching me with a smile as If I were her treat what she'd been looking for. And I felt like smiling too as it was the only logical thing I felt to do in that moment. She was wearing a pink dress and with her wobbly tiny steps towards me made me realise that she recently knew how to walk as she could't walk in one straight line. I was sitting on a bench and there was ample space for her to do things and she was persistently trying to get onto that to sit with me but couldn't and at one point I felt like giving her hand to help but I respected her dad who was vigilantly looking at her from distance. She went back for a minute as she was told that it's time to go home but she wanted more to play. I felt it was just a one off moments like in the past as kids did find me friendly to hang out with so I kinda stepped back again to my pensive state. But to my surprise she came towards me again but this time with the highest pace as much as her tiny legs could allow and then she stopped in front of to me to and showed me her closed fist, she rotated slowly as if she learnt it from a movie but to my surprise it was empty. I genuinely smiled at the moment and she giggled seeing me accepting her gift to me. She went back as this time her dad wanted her to go home right away. 

I couldn't understand for a second what just happened to me, as I was just sitting on the bench being clueless as once she went out of my sight, I look back to my hand in which she tried to put something there and then after a fractions of seconds I felt myself got moved to tears, I felt she gave me hope as she already knew what I was dealing with and guided me to be brave when I lose my way. I just wanted to hold onto that feeling which I was experiencing at that moment as it felt like "a call" from a mystical place which many affirms as a sign of God, for some it's just the destiny or karma but for me it's a transcendent place which I can never perceive, neither process with my biological tools, and even worse can't talk about it as it's beyond linguistic capabilities but only be felt at times. I've had a vague sense that this to exist somewhere and what the little girl did was to give me one another hope for me to find that place. To human's understanding, maybe it's the place which I want to build for myself with utmost care and honesty and call it a home, and the nonphysical thing that girl left it in my hand was the hope to be brave to keep looking forward to that.
