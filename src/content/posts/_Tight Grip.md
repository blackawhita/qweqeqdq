## Order and Chaos

Order is something that you are familiar with, and do regularly and after giving repetition, feels to happen automatically. You wake up at a certain time and clear your teeth before eating breakfast. It's something already explored by you and brain has registered the mapping so that it'll as frictionless as possible. Frictionless being the most important aspect of having something in order. The inertia you feel writing with your non dominant hand is something we all familiar with, but after some work, it'll get easier.

Chaos on the other hand something that is unknown, unexplored, and unpredictable and lies outside the map of. It's novelty surprises and you don't know where it could lead you. It's like saying yes to something which you can't really foresee the ending. Talking about friction, here you find it in every corner trying to make it difficult for you. 

Just like Order, Chaos is equally important. The ones which are now considered to be in Order were Chaos in the past.
